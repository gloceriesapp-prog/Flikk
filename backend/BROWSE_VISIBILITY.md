# Browse visibility and purchase eligibility

Approved catalogue entries remain browseable when a shop closes or a product sells out. Temporary availability does not delete cards, change collection membership, or remove category/search results. Existing address scopes, manual admin selections, enabled sections, approval checks, row limits and pagination still apply. This change does not fabricate unavailable festival stock or resurrect deleted/unapproved listings.

Customer inventory hooks now expose `visibleProducts`, including closed shops. Product mapping carries the public opening schedule and owner's active flag. A shared local 30-second clock updates card availability and nearby shop labels without database polling. Stock remains a separate state: confirmed depletion shows **Out of stock**, unconfirmed inventory shows **Stock updating**, and a closed shop with otherwise eligible inventory shows **Shop closed**. Card, deal and product-sheet Add/quantity controls are replaced by this status until eligible again. Existing cart items remain present; authoritative checkout quotes and final transactional eligibility checks still reject closed shops, sold-out quantities, unapproved products and invalid delivery addresses.

Admin **Available packs** (`/inventory-pack-stock`) is accessible from the sidebar and Inventory. Confirm actual sellable counts per database pack. Its existing service-only stock RPC updates counts, tracking, stock status and the in-stock flag together. Legacy rows marked in-stock but without confirmed counts must not be enabled by inventing quantities. Read-only aggregate diagnostics on 2026-10-05 found 10 approved listings with tracking disabled and all 11 pack quantities unconfirmed; this explains their unavailable state even though their legacy status said in-stock.

Migration `093_persistent_browse_visibility.sql` upgrades only catalogue selectors: collection previews/pages, search/category facets, popularity and repeat-purchase candidates. It keeps all selection and pagination caps and adds an approved-product store/ID index that includes sold-out rows. RPCs remain service-only. API hydration and legacy product feeds retain approval and store/location scoping while removing temporary stock/open-state filters.

Deploy migration 093 before the updated backend/client. It was applied and tested only in the isolated local database, not the live project. `backend/tests/sql/browse-visibility.sql` is guarded to the disposable full-schema fixture and rolls back its test rows. It verifies closed/sold-out preview, paged collection and search visibility, plus unapproved-product exclusion. TypeScript tests verify UI status, preservation of product metadata and parity with backend scheduled-hour checks. Actual customer-device appearance remains to be reviewed on iOS/Android.
